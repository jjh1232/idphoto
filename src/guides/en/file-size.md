"File too large" is the most common upload error with ID photos.
A phone photo is usually 2–5 MB; upload forms often cap the photo at a few hundred kilobytes, and US visa and DV Lottery photos must be 240 KB or less.

## What decides file size

Two things:

1. **Pixel count** — width × height.
2. **JPEG quality** — how carefully those pixels are stored.

An ID photo's pixel count is set by its size rule, so the part you can actually change is the **quality**.
Advice to "make the image smaller" by cutting pixels does shrink the file, but it breaks the required dimensions and makes prints blurry.

## Keep the size, shrink the file

1. Open the [ID photo cropper](../en), add your photo, pick the size and place the frame.
2. Under **File size limit**, choose the cap — 100, 200, 240 or 500 KB.
3. Press **Download**. It finds the highest quality that still fits, and saves that.

No need to step the quality down by hand. The tool narrows it down by halves and keeps the best value under the limit.
After saving, it shows the pixel size, the file size and the quality that was used.

## Roughly what to expect

A 2×2 photo at 300 dpi is 600×600 pixels, and at normal quality it usually lands around 100 KB — comfortably under 240 KB.
Most "too large" errors come from uploading the **original phone photo**. Cropping to size alone takes megabytes down to tens or hundreds of kilobytes.

The caps on offer are 100, 200, 240 and 500 KB. If a form asks for something smaller, such as 50 KB, the tool cannot target it yet — 300 dpi with the 100 KB cap is the closest it gets.

## If it still will not fit

If you see "Even at the lowest quality it is __ KB, which does not fit":

- **Switch from 600 dpi to 300 dpi.** That cuts the pixel count to a quarter and the file size with it.
- Busy or noisy backgrounds (photos taken in dim light) compress badly. A retake in good light against a plain wall is often quicker.

When the limit cannot be met, the tool tells you instead of handing over a half-shrunk file — better to find out here than at the upload page.

## Check the form's own rules

Size caps, pixel requirements and accepted formats differ between sites. Read the upload instructions first.
For US visas and the DV Lottery: JPEG, 600×600 pixels or more, 240 KB or less. For online passport renewal the State Department accepts larger files, up to 10 MB.
